Due Diligence

What do laws and policies permit you to do with a site? What is an appropriate market price for the land? What are the risks, and how can they be mitigated? Our team undertakes due diligence for land acquisition.

Visioning

Designers and engineers require direction to do great work. Our skilled staff envision the possibilities for placemaking to guide land development projects. We bring placemaking dreams to reality.

Project Management

We manage complex interdisciplinary teams to bring land development projects from first inception to the Building Permit stage. We partner with our sister company, Natcon Construction Services, to provide construction management services.

04

Approvals Strategy and Advocacy

Checking boxes on a development application form is not enough to ensure that projects are approved by local government to proceed. The Natland Group draws upon decades of local knowledge to craft approval strategies to mitigate risk and earn project approval. We engage with communities, municipal staff and politicians to create successful projects.
